Entry for the [Worst Superpower](https://make.fun/c/u-suroto-worst-superpower-461dcdbe)
bounty on make.fun: invent the most useless superpower in one line of at most
140 characters. A judge model ranks the field two at a time; the single
funniest, most useless invention takes the whole $2 pool.

## The entry

> I can turn completely invisible, but only when absolutely no one is looking at me.

The power is self-defeating by construction: the one condition under which
invisibility would matter — someone watching — is exactly the condition that
switches it off. It is useless in every situation where it could be useful,
and useful only where nobody would ever notice.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only, deterministic) writes the one-liner
to `/output/entry.txt`. The line is a single line of well under 140
characters, plain UTF-8 text.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
